Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026.

Enigma: Party Trivia doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, the names and faces you pick, and your Passport progress are saved only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing together

When you play in the same room, the phones find each other over your local network or Bluetooth and talk directly. The names, answers and scores for the game go only to the other phones in that game. When you play over SharePlay, that information goes through Apple's FaceTime and SharePlay service to the phones in the call, under Apple's privacy policy. None of it comes to us.

Children

Enigma is for families. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Questions: send us a message.
