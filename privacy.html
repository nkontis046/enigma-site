Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026.

We don't collect, store, see, share or sell any data about you. None at all.

Enigma: Party Trivia has:

  • no accounts, sign-ups or logins
  • no analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or usage tracking
  • no advertising and no ad identifiers
  • no tracking across apps or websites
  • no servers of ours, so we have nowhere to receive or keep your data
  • no in-app purchases, and no payment details ever reach us
  • no use of your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone

Apple's App Store privacy label for the app is “Data Not Collected”.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, the names and faces you pick, and your Passport progress are saved only on your own device. We never receive them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Playing together

When you play in the same room, the phones find each other over your local network or Bluetooth and talk directly. The names, answers and scores for the game go only to the other phones in that game, and nothing is kept after it ends except on each phone. When you play over SharePlay, that information goes through Apple's FaceTime and SharePlay service to the phones in the call, under Apple's privacy policy. None of it comes to us.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Those are controlled by Apple and by you, and they don't identify you. You can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Enigma is for families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
