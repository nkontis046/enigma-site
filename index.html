A trivia night for the whole room.

One phone hosts and reads the questions aloud. Everyone else joins on their own phone and plays.

Nearly 3,000 questions. Science, music, animals, history, sport and much more, at three levels of difficulty.
Play together, or on your own. Play as players or teams in the same room, over SharePlay with people far away, or solo against computer regulars.
English and Spanish. The questions, the voice and the whole app.
No accounts, no ads, no data collected. The phones talk to each other directly. Nothing about you leaves the room.
