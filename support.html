Common questions

The other phones can't find the game. Put every phone on the same Wi-Fi, or turn Bluetooth on. The first time you open Enigma, iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. If you said no, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Enigma.

Can we play with people who aren't in the room? Yes. Start a FaceTime call, then host an online game. It runs over SharePlay.

A phone dropped out mid-game. Open Enigma again on that phone and rejoin. It gets its seat and its score back. If the host phone closes, reopen it and choose to carry on.

The voice sounds robotic. Download an enhanced voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, and Enigma will use it.

Can I play in Spanish? Yes. Change the language in the game's settings, or set your phone to Spanish.

Does it cost anything to keep playing? No. There are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases.
